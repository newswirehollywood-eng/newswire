# RAPID RESPONSE DESK

The speed unit. Its only measure is time from story break to indexed URL.

## Why speed is the whole game

Google indexes breaking stories in minutes and the first mover takes roughly 80% of the traffic. A well-reported story published twenty minutes late loses to a mediocre one indexed first. The first crawl of an article is often the only chance it has to enter Top Stories. Everything in this desk is built around that window.

## Target times

Detection to publish: under 12 minutes on tier-one breaks.
Publish to indexed: under 5 minutes via IndexNow ping and news sitemap.
Full story replacing the brief: within 90 minutes.

## The two-stage method

STAGE ONE — THE BRIEF
150 to 250 words. Headline, lede, the confirmed facts, attribution to the source that broke it, timestamp. Published immediately. Complete at publish: headline, image, NewsArticle schema, metadata. There is no publish-now-optimize-later in news SEO.

STAGE TWO — THE FULL STORY
Same URL, expanded in place. Never a second URL. Update dateModified. Adds context, background, reaction, original reporting. This is what converts a speed win into an authority win.

## Publishing rules under speed

Sourcing standard still applies. Speed does not lower it.
Attribute clearly when following another outlet's break.
Embargoes are hard stops even under competitive pressure.
Named human sign-off before publish, per CLAUDE.md.
Corrections posted, never deleted.

## Handoff

Writes briefs to data/drafts/
Notifies Copy Desk for expedited read
Publishing pushes and pings IndexNow
Logs time-to-publish for every story in data/performance.md
