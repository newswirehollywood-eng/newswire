# GLOBAL DESK

Newswire Hollywood as an international source, not an American one covering international stories.

## Territories

United Kingdom: London, BAFTA, London Film Festival, London Fashion Week, UK production and streaming
France: Cannes, Paris Fashion Week, French production and co-production, Unifrance
Japan: Tokyo International Film Festival, anime and IP licensing, Japanese streaming and music
Korea and wider Asia: Busan, Korean content export, K-music and film
United Arab Emirates: Dubai, Red Sea and Gulf financing, festivals, brand and luxury activations
Nigeria and Africa: Nollywood, African streaming expansion, Pan African Film Festival ties
Latin America: Mexico and Brazil production, Spanish-language streaming

## Why this is a revenue position, not just coverage

International entertainment finance, Gulf-backed production, and Asian IP licensing sit closer to business and finance coverage than to consumer entertainment. Business and finance content carries materially higher advertising rates than pop culture. Global industry coverage is priced as trade inventory.

International audiences also make the syndication and wire lines viable in both directions: we license out, and foreign outlets license in.

## Language

Priority order for translated editions: Spanish, French, Japanese, Arabic, Korean, Portuguese.
Implement with hreflang tags so search engines serve the right edition per territory.
Translate the evergreen and calendar pages first. They hold value; breaking briefs do not.
Machine translation is acceptable for calendar and listings. Editorial features get human review before publication.

## Global ambassadors and correspondents

Named contributors in territory who file, attend events, and hold local credentials.
Structure: contributor agreement, byline credit, per-piece or revenue share rather than salary.
Ambassadors are also relationship assets, since they carry local publicist and organizer contacts we cannot build from Los Angeles.

Target: one contributor each in London, Paris, Tokyo, Seoul, Dubai, Lagos, Mexico City.

## Global calendar scope

The events calendar runs worldwide, not just Los Angeles.
Fashion weeks: New York, London, Milan, Paris, Tokyo, Seoul, Dubai.
Festivals: Cannes, Venice, Berlin, Busan, Tokyo, Marrakech, Red Sea, Toronto.
Awards: BAFTA, César, Japan Academy, Blue Dragon, International Emmys.
Markets: MIPCOM, MIPTV, EFM, AFM, Content London, Asia TV Forum.
Conferences and summits with entertainment industry attendance.

Markets matter disproportionately. They are where the check cutters gather, badges are purchasable, and credentialing is straightforward compared with red carpets.

## Partnership targets

International press associations and foreign press corps in Los Angeles
Foreign film commissions and national film bodies with LA offices
Consulates with cultural programs, which run galas and fund coverage
Festival press offices, which credential international press more readily than domestic

## Trigger

Weekly sweep per territory.
Manual: run the global desk.
