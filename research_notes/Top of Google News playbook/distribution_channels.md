# Distribution Channels Beyond Google (research notes, as of Nov 21, 2026)

Scope: aggregators, wire/press-release distribution, social-to-search loops, newsletters/push, and partnerships, for a small independent publisher group (Newswire Hollywood, Hollywood News Access, Gospel News Access, World Newswire Press, PR STARPOWER).

Method note: 17 searches/fetches. Two primary sources could not be fetched because the session network blocked them (presswire.com pricing table; pressgazette.co.uk on Meta One link limits); their content below comes from search-result snippets only and should be re-checked once those hosts are allowed. Many 2026 figures come from vendor/affiliate blogs and are flagged as such.

Status legend: OPEN = public application; INVITE = invite-only or closed to unsolicited applications; UNCLEAR = conflicting evidence.

## Aggregators: which accept small publishers, and how

### Takeaway
The big "default" aggregators (MSN, Apple News, Yahoo News syndication, Google News Showcase) are effectively closed or invite-only to new small publishers in 2026; the realistic open doors are NewsBreak (if US-focused with bylines/About page), SmartNews (application + SmartFormat feed, selective), Flipboard (RSS submit, reviewed), Yahoo Creators (individual creator track, 50/50 ad share, waitlist), and Perplexity's Comet Plus pool. Plain Google News inclusion needs no application at all.

### Cited Findings

**MSN / Microsoft Start Partner Hub — INVITE (possibly paused)**
- Microsoft's own setup page: "MSN Partner Hub is invitation-only. To sign up as an MSN Partner, you will need the unique Invite code that was emailed to you by Microsoft." — [Microsoft Support: How to set up your MSN Partner Hub account](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/msn/partner-hub/how-to-set-up-your-msn-partner-hub-account)
- An October 2026 Microsoft Q&A thread says onboarding of new partners is "temporarily paused while recruitment is updated," with no reopening date — but this was an AI-generated forum answer, so unverified. — [Microsoft Q&A: How can I apply for MSN Partner Hub Program?](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5850858/how-can-i-apply-for-msn-partner-hub-program); see also [Request for MSN Partner Hub Invite code](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/6042062/request-for-msn-partner-hub-invite-code)
- A secondary post describes an "Apply for an invite code" link on the Partner Hub page; it says applying "means waiting for an update, not guaranteed access," and lists prerequisites: an About page naming publisher and owner, reader contact details, and legal rights to everything published. — [PublisherBox on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@publisherbox/post/DfC77tkDVAx/signing-in-to-msn-partner-hub-does-not-get-your-publication-onto-msn-you-need/)
- Alternative route: content can reach MSN via a content partner listed in Microsoft's official directory rather than your own account. — [air.io: How to get YouTube videos on MSN in 2026](https://air.io/en/on-air/how-to-join-msn-to-spread-your-content)
- Microsoft publishes an official "MSN Partner Hub" WordPress plugin (for partners who already have access). — [WordPress.org plugin page](https://wordpress.org/plugins/microsoft-start/)
- A third-party guide claims you can self-apply and submit a feed; this conflicts with Microsoft's documentation. — [Feed Fusion guide](https://www.feedfusion.net/publisher-guides/become-msn-microsoft-start-publisher/); contradicted by [Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/msn/partner-hub/how-to-set-up-your-msn-partner-hub-account)

**Apple News — INVITE / UNCLEAR for new applicants**
- 2021 News Partner Program: open to Apple Developer Program members; requires a license addendum; applications at developer.apple.com/programs/news-partner; targets subscription publications delivering in Apple News Format (ANF); 15% commission on qualifying in-app subscriptions from day one (vs standard 30% year one). — [Apple Newsroom, Aug 2021](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/08/apple-introduces-the-news-partner-program/)
- A 2026 guide reports that in April 2025 a local outlet posted on Apple's developer forum that Apple support told it "Apple News is no longer accepting unsolicited applications"; guide says there is no open sign-up for new publishers (secondary source). — [Publisher in a Box: Apple News for Publishers](https://publisherinabox.com/apple-news-for-publishers)
- Apple describes News as a platform for professional journalistic publications based in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia (per secondary guide). — [Publisher in a Box](https://publisherinabox.com/apple-news-for-publishers)

**Yahoo News — publisher syndication INVITE; Yahoo Creators OPEN with waitlist**
- No public application exists for publisher syndication; Yahoo hires "Editorial Partner Manager" staff to manage external partnerships, implying direct outreach. Digiday says publisher syndication content "remains crucial" to Yahoo News. — [Digiday: Yahoo offers more editorial control to creators](https://digiday.com/media/yahoo-takes-cues-from-platforms-as-it-offers-more-editorial-control-to-creators); [Built In job listing](https://builtin.com/job/editorial-partner-manager-curation/4561237)
- Yahoo for Creators launched in beta March 14, 2024: creators publish stories across Yahoo News. — [Yahoo Inc. press release](https://www.yahooinc.com/press/introducing-yahoo-for-creators-a-new-publishing-platform-for-creators-launching-in-beta)
- Terms: "competitive 50/50 ad revenue share"; meeting criteria "does not guarantee acceptance." — [Yahoo Creators FAQ](https://webflow.creators.yahooinc.com/faq)
- Waitlist of creators; no content production quotas; June 2025 was the program's highest revenue/engagement month (no figures shared). — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/yahoo-takes-cues-from-platforms-as-it-offers-more-editorial-control-to-creators)

**NewsBreak — OPEN (US-focused publishers), plus Contributor Network for individuals**
- Apply at publishers.newsbreak.com ("Apply to be a Partner — not all publishers may qualify"). — [NewsBreak Publishers](https://publishers.newsbreak.com/)
- Works with editorial publishers creating original text/video; prioritizes local news; individual bloggers/freelancers are directed to the Contributor Network. — [NewsBreak: Who is qualified](https://help.newsbreak.com/hc/en-us/articles/36837077805709-Who-is-qualified-for-NewsBreak-s-Publisher-Partnership-program)
- Common rejection reasons: non-US or not US-focused; no About Us page or editorial staff info; missing bylines or image credits; mostly aggregated content. After approval: accept ToS, create publisher profile, upload RSS feed(s). — [NewsBreak Publisher FAQ](https://support.newsbreak.com/publisher-faq); [Application review criteria](https://support.newsbreak.com/knowledge/application-review-process-and-approval-criteria)
- Caution: critics say the ToS grants a very broad content license and NewsBreak's in-app format strips publisher ads, email/subscription CTAs and recirculation. — [Nearview Media: Publishers should be wary of NewsBreak](https://nearviewmedia.com/newsbreak/); [E-Moxie](https://emoxie.com/publishers-should-be-wary-of-newsbreak/)

**SmartNews — OPEN application, selective**
- Apply via partner page; applications "reviewed extensively" and judged on "the current content needs of our product"; must include a feed that validates in SmartNews's validator (SmartFormat, an enhanced RSS). — [SmartNews: Partner with SmartNews](https://about.smartnews.com/en/press/publishers.html)
- 2026 guide: feed must carry full article text; no published revenue share on partner page. — [Feed Fusion: SmartNews guide 2026](https://feedfusion.net/publisher-guides/become-smartnews-publisher/); [Publisher in a Box: SmartNews 2026](https://publisherinabox.com/smartnews-for-publishers-2026)

**Flipboard — OPEN (RSS submission, human review)**
- Apply at flipboard.com/publishers, create/claim a magazine, submit RSS under "Sources"; reviewed by Flipboard staff, not instant or guaranteed (secondary source). — [PostRSS](https://postrss.com/can-you-add-an-rss-feed-to-a-flipboard-magazine-whats-actually-possible/)

**Google News / Showcase**
- Google stopped accepting manual Publisher Center submissions in April 2024 and moved to auto-generated publication pages by late March 2025; inclusion depends on compliance with Google News content policies. — [Patrick Stox: Google News SEO](https://patrickstox.com/technical-seo/news-seo/google-news-seo/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-news-shifts-to-automated-publication-pages/)
- Showcase is a separate paid licensing program; placement not guaranteed. A July 2026 report says Google is moving Showcase publishers to an "AI-powered article overviews" pilot with future fees conditional on AI training rights (single report, unconfirmed). — [Android Headlines, Jul 2026](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/07/google-forces-publishers-ai-training-rights-news-showcase.html)

**Perplexity / AI news**
- Perplexity Publishers' Program launched July 2024 as ad revenue share; ads funding it ended Feb 2026. Successor Comet Plus (announced Aug 2025): $5/month, $42.5M initial pool, 80% of revenue to participating publishers, distributed by visits, citations and agent actions. No payout totals disclosed as of Oct 31, 2026. — [Keywords Everywhere: Perplexity updates 2026](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/perplexity-updates/); [AEO Daily](https://aeo-daily.com/perplexity-publisher-program)
- CNN reportedly sued Perplexity in May 2026 after a payment deal ended; claim of 2,400+ enrolled publishers is unverified. — [AEO Daily](https://aeo-daily.com/perplexity-publisher-program); [Tech Jacks Solutions](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-tools/perplexity/perplexity-publisher-program/)

### Inferences
- Fastest realistic wins for Newswire Hollywood: NewsBreak (US entertainment news qualifies if bylines, image credits, About/staff pages exist), Flipboard (RSS), SmartNews (requires a full-text SmartFormat feed — a wire.py output job), Yahoo Creators (Terry personally, as creator). Gospel News Access and Hollywood News Access are similarly eligible; World Newswire Press may hit NewsBreak's "US-focused" rejection rule.
- PR STARPOWER (a PR operation) is unlikely to qualify as an editorial publisher on any aggregator; its content belongs on paid wires.
- MSN and Apple: apply for invite code / developer program anyway, but plan as if closed; MSN content-partner intermediaries are the workaround.
- NewsBreak license terms conflict with the "monetize everything" rule; read ToS before accepting.

### Gaps
- Opera News, Ground News, Feedly, Upday/Samsung News, Particle, and Pocket successor: not researched within the tool budget; no findings.
- OpenAI/ChatGPT publisher program terms for small publishers: no sources found.
- Traffic expectations per aggregator: no reliable published figures found for small publishers.
- Apple News Format requirement vs RSS for new 2026 applicants: unconfirmed.

## Wire and press-release distribution: prices, reach, and becoming a syndication source

### Takeaway
Paid press-release wires range from ~$100 (EIN Presswire) to ~$475–$1,145+ per 400-word US release (Business Wire, PR Newswire); prices are mostly unpublished and vary by source. AP and Reuters do not have a public contributor door for small publishers; the realistic syndication-source route is marketplaces like Reuters Connect (points-trading) or direct deals.

### Cited Findings
- EIN Presswire: from $99.95 per release; broader packages to $499.95; other sources say $149 single, $66.60/release in a 15-pack. — [Jerusalem Post: Best 15 PR distribution services 2026](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/article-898710); [Reporter Outreach: Press release cost 2026](https://www.reporteroutreach.com/blog/press-release-cost)
- PR Newswire: no price on own site; its 2026 guide lists a US national release at $1,145 for 400 words; another source says $850; range cited as ~$350–$8,699 plus ~$195+ annual membership. — [Pressonify 2026 comparison](https://pressonify.ai/blog/press-release-distribution-pricing-comparison-2026); [RhinoRank](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/press-release-distribution-cost/); [presswire.com pricing table (snippet only; fetch blocked)](https://presswire.com/press-release-distribution-pricing/)
- Business Wire: own pricing page shows "$475 starting price for 400 words, U.S."; other estimates ~$940 national. — [presswire.com (snippet)](https://presswire.com/press-release-distribution-pricing/); [Subscribe PR](https://subscribepr.com/blog/which-press-release-distribution-service-is-best/)
- GlobeNewswire: no published rate card; estimates $350–$1,200 or $600–$1,500 US national. — [RhinoRank](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/press-release-distribution-cost/); [PBJ Stories](https://www.pbjstories.com/blog/press-release-cost)
- ACCESS Newswire (formerly Accesswire): subscription, listed from $934/month. Newswire.com and eReleases start ~$399; PRWeb ~$120–$245. — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/article-898710); [PR News.io](https://prnews.io/blog/pr-distribution-software.html)
- All above figures come from vendor/affiliate blogs, not rate cards (except Business Wire's $475); treat as estimates dated 2026.
- Reuters Connect: B2B marketplace; ~a quarter of content from partners; publishers earn points when their content is downloaded and spend them on others' content; 60+ news orgs in 2019. — [Digiday: Reuters marketplace](https://digiday.com/media/reuters-building-marketplace-video-hungry-publishers); [Thomson Reuters 2019 release](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2019/november/with-new-partnerships-reuters-connect-becomes-the-most-comprehensive-digital-platform-powering-the-news-ecosystem)
- Gannett + Reuters bundled content via Reuters API and Connect (Jan 2025). — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250102601469/en)
- AP Content Services is AP's branded-content arm, not a contributor program. — [Business Wire partner page](https://secure.businesswire.com/partners/ap-content-services)

### Inferences
- For PR STARPOWER client work, EIN Presswire is the budget tier; Business Wire's $475 starting tier is the only verifiable premium price. Pass costs through to clients and log them in the Work Ledger.
- Becoming a syndication source: Reuters Connect is the documented mechanism, but only for established news orgs; small publishers are more likely to win direct deals with aggregators (NewsBreak, Yahoo) than with wires.

### Gaps
- PRLog and openPR (free/low-cost) prices and Google News pickup: not researched.
- Which wires actually reach Google News, Yahoo Finance, AP: no verified source found (vendor claims only).
- AP stringer/freelance process and AP member terms: no sources found.

## Social-to-search loops

### Takeaway
Google now indexes public Instagram professional-account posts (since July 10, 2025) and TikTok/X content, and Discover surfaces X, Instagram and YouTube Shorts posts from followed creators, so social posts are themselves search inventory. Facebook link posts get very little reach (1.1% of US feed views had outside links, Q2 2026). YouTube "Top news" eligibility hinges on following Google News content policies, with "authoritative" sources favored.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram began allowing Google and other search engines to index public posts from professional (business/creator, 18+) accounts on July 10, 2025; setting under Settings → Privacy → "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results"; Stories not indexed; opt-in vs default-on disputed. — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagram-posts-in-google-search-what-creators-need-to-know/); [WERSM](https://wersm.com/instagram-content-will-now-be-searchable-on-google/); [Boderia](https://www.boderia.io/insights/demand-and-marketing/google-is-indexing-instagram-posts-what-you-must-know)
- Google has indexed TikTok videos since 2023; X content also appears in search (general claims, secondary). — [Proof3](https://proof3.co/insights/google-indexing-instagram-posts); [Media a la Carte](https://www.mediaalacarte.com/post/google-is-now-indexing-your-instagram-posts-and-what-to-do-about-it)
- YouTube Top News shelf appears on searches for specific stories and "can't be dismissed"; section appears "if we have relevant content from news sources that follow Google News content policies." — [YouTube Help: News on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9057101?hl=en); [YouTube docs cited in Supreme Court filing](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/URLs_Cited/OT2023/22-277/22-277-17.pdf)
- 2023: YouTube's Top News and Breaking News shelves and new news watch page show only "authoritative" news sources (criteria not spelled out). — [NiemanLab, Oct 2023](https://www.niemanlab.org/2023/10/youtube-launches-new-watch-page-that-only-shows-videos-from-authoritative-news-sources/); [YouTube Blog: Four Rs](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/the-four-rs-of-responsibility-raise-and-reduce/)
- Facebook: only 1.1% of US Feed views were posts with outside links in Q2 2026 (Meta Widely Viewed Content data, as cited); Similarweb shows Facebook traffic down 5% YoY across top 10 publishers; Reuters Institute Jan 2026 found substantial falls in Facebook and X referrals over three years. — [Digiday: Publishers furious over 'Meta Zero'](https://digiday.com/media/they-hold-us-hostage-publishers-furious-over-terms-change-still-cant-quit-facebook/); [Press Gazette (snippet; fetch blocked)](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/facebook-cash-for-links-scheme-excludes-publishers-for-now/)
- Meta One: Facebook now limits free external link posts for Pages per month (paid beyond); news publishers are excluded "for now." — [Press Gazette (snippet)](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/facebook-cash-for-links-scheme-excludes-publishers-for-now/); [Publisher in a Box: Meta One](https://publisherinabox.com/facebook-link-limits-meta-one-what-publishers-do)
- Contrary claim: some publishers saw Facebook referrals quadruple vs March 2025 (affiliate source, unverified). — [Yepads](https://yepads.com/facebook-algorithm-changes/)

### Inferences
- Switch Instagram accounts for all five brands to professional and turn on search indexing: each post becomes a Google-rankable page at zero cost.
- On Facebook, native video/photo with link in comment is likely better than link posts; verify whether Newswire Hollywood qualifies as a "news publisher" under Meta One exemption.
- YouTube Top News requires Google News policy compliance (already a site goal); post news clips fast with clear titles.

### Gaps
- Threads, Bluesky, WhatsApp Channels and Telegram traffic data for news: no sources found.
- Meta One exact link limit and price: snippet only, not confirmed.
- Canada Meta news block current status: not researched.
- Best posting times/formats per platform: no reliable data found.

## Newsletters and push

### Takeaway
Google's Preferred Sources button (Aug 20, 2026) and Discover "Follow" (Sept 2025) are free, direct ways to lock in Google repeat exposure. OneSignal web push is free for unlimited subscribers but caps sends at 10,000 subscribers per send on the free plan.

### Cited Findings
- Discover Follow launched Sept 2025; signed-in users follow publishers/creators from a Discover card; followed sources' X, Instagram and YouTube Shorts posts can appear in the feed. — [Google blog: Personalize Search, News, Discover](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/); [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/google-discover-publisher-pages-links-featured-posts/)
- Preferred Sources: US/India rollout Aug 2025; all languages April 2026; extended to AI Overviews/AI Mode mid-2026; embeddable publisher button released Aug 20, 2026 (code in Search Central docs); Google says people are twice as likely to click a preferred source. — [TechCrunch, Aug 20, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/google-gives-publishers-a-new-way-to-fight-ai-driven-traffic-losses/); [arwriterai guide](https://arwriterai.com/en/blog/google-preferred-sources-button-publishers-2026/)
- Preferred Sources works at domain/subdomain level, not subdirectory (secondary). — [arwriterai](https://arwriterai.com/en/blog/google-preferred-sources-button-publishers-2026/)
- OneSignal free plan: unlimited web push subscribers and messages, but only 10,000 subscribers per send; from Sept 1/Oct 1, 2026 mobile push/in-app capped at 1,000 MAU, web push and email unaffected; Growth plan from $19/month plus $0.004 per web push subscriber. — [OneSignal: Understanding pricing](https://onesignal.com/blog/understanding-onesignals-pricing/); [Notifly](https://notifly.io/compare/onesignal/pricing)

### Inferences
- Add the Preferred Sources button to every article template in wire.py (free, one-time engineering task); each brand on its own domain counts separately.
- OneSignal free tier fits until a brand passes 10,000 push subscribers.

### Gaps
- Substack vs beehiiv 2026 pricing and referral/recommendation features: not found.
- Google News "Follow" button status in 2026: not confirmed separately from Preferred Sources.

## Partnerships

### Takeaway
Syndication with Yahoo/MSN is handled by partner managers through direct outreach, not open applications; small publishers enter via creator tracks (Yahoo Creators) or open feeds (NewsBreak, SmartNews, Flipboard).

### Cited Findings
- Yahoo employs editorial partner managers for external Yahoo News partnerships. — [Built In](https://builtin.com/job/editorial-partner-manager-curation/4561237)
- Reuters Connect trades content for points among partner news orgs. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/reuters-building-marketplace-video-hungry-publishers)
- Marie Claire publisher Future launched its own creator program (Sept 2025), a model of publishers building creator networks. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/23/marie-claire-future-collab-creator-program)

### Inferences
- Approach: build traffic and Google News compliance first, then pitch Yahoo/MSN partner managers with numbers.

### Gaps
- Local TV partnership models and guest byline programs: not researched.
