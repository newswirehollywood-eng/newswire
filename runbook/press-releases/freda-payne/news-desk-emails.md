Freda Payne sings for veterans Jan. 8 at Mullett Arena, Tempe: photos and interview availability

## Body (copy from here)

Hello,

"Band of Gold" singer Freda Payne, 84, will perform at the Motown for Veterans Benefit Concert on Friday, Jan. 8, 2027, at Mullett Arena in Tempe, Ariz., with the Former Ladies of the Supremes, Glenn Leonard's Temptations Revue, Jr. Walker's All-Star Band and Alli Starr. Doors 6 p.m., show 7 p.m.

Proceeds benefit Green Homes for Vets, a 501(c)(3) building an eco-friendly home community in Prescott Valley for returning combat veterans.

The angle: in 1971 Payne recorded "Bring the Boys Home," a Vietnam-era plea to bring soldiers home. Fifty-five years later she is singing to help house the ones who made it back.
