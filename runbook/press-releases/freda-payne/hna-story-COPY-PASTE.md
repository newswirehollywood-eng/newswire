HEADLINE: Freda Payne, 84, Is Still Booking Arenas: "Band of Gold" Legend Joins Former Supremes for Veterans Benefit Jan. 8

DEK: The Detroit-born singer shares the Mullett Arena stage in Tempe with the Former Ladies of the Supremes and Glenn Leonard's Temptations Revue, and the money goes to homes for combat veterans.

Freda Payne is 84 and her calendar does not look like it.

After a sold-out September tribute show at Catalina Jazz Club in Hollywood and an October run at sea aboard Holland America with Darlene Love and Wayne Newton, the "Band of Gold" singer is booked for the Motown for Veterans Benefit Concert on Friday, Jan. 8, 2027, at Mullett Arena in Tempe, Ariz.

She shares the bill with the Former Ladies of the Supremes, Glenn Leonard's Temptations Revue, Jr. Walker's All-Star Band and Alli Starr. Doors open at 6 p.m.; the show starts at 7.

Proceeds benefit Green Homes for Vets, a 501(c)(3) building an eco-friendly home community in Prescott Valley, Ariz., for combat service men and women coming home.

There is history here. In 1971 Payne recorded "Bring the Boys Home," a Vietnam-era plea to bring American soldiers home. Fifty-five years later she is singing to help house the veterans who made it back.

Tickets are on Ticketmaster, listed from $89 to $235, with VIP meet-and-greet packages and an online silent auction also offered:
