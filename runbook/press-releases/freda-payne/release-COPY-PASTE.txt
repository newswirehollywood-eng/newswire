Freda Payne to Perform at Motown for Veterans Benefit Concert Jan. 8 in Tempe, Ariz.

The “Band of Gold” singer joins the Former Ladies of the Supremes, Glenn Leonard’s Temptations Revue and Jr. Walker’s All-Star Band on Jan. 8 at Mullett Arena in Tempe, Ariz. Proceeds go to Green Homes for Vets.

LOS ANGELES (Nov. 19, 2026) — Freda Payne, the Detroit-born singer whose “Band of Gold” became a worldwide hit in 1970, will perform at the Motown for Veterans Benefit Concert on Friday, Jan. 8, 2027, at Mullett Arena in Tempe, Ariz., PR STARPOWER announced today.

Payne shares the bill with the Former Ladies of the Supremes, Glenn Leonard’s Temptations Revue, Jr. Walker’s All-Star Band and Alli Starr. Doors open at 6 p.m. and the show begins at 7 p.m.

Proceeds benefit Green Homes for Vets, a 501(c)(3) organization working to build an eco-friendly home community in Prescott Valley, Ariz., for combat service men and women returning home.

Tickets are on sale now through Ticketmaster (https://www.ticketmaster.com/motown-for-veterans-benefit-concert-tempe-arizona-01-08-2027/event/19006522B3C069C6), listed from $89 to $235. Premium suites and loge upgrades are available, along with VIP meet-and-greet and sponsorship packages and an online silent auction, according to the event’s listings. Details: mullettarena.com/event/motown (https://mullettarena.com/event/motown/).

A legend’s season

The Tempe date caps a busy fall for Payne, 84. On Sept. 26 she played to a sold-out Catalina Jazz Club in Los Angeles with Freda Payne Portrays and Honors Legendary Ladies of Song, written and directed by Donald B. Welch, a night Newswire Hollywood’s review called “not a tribute show” but “testimony.” In late October she performed at sea aboard Holland America, on a Fort Lauderdale-to-Puerto Rico sailing that also featured Darlene Love and Wayne Newton.

About Freda Payne

Freda Payne is a singer and actress from Detroit whose career spans six decades. Her 1970 single “Band of Gold” became an international hit, and she followed it with “Bring the Boys Home,” her 1971 anti-war song of the Vietnam era. A two-time Grammy nominee, she has worked across jazz, pop, R&B and the stage. Her memoir, Band of Gold: A Memoir, is available now. Official website: fredapayne.net (https://www.fredapayne.net/).
