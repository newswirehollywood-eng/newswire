HEADLINE: Inside Tevin Campbell's 50th: A Phoenix Invite, a Packed Room, and the Birthday Boy Sang

DEK: The "Can We Talk" star turned 50 and threw a party to match. Then he grabbed the mic. What would YOUR 50th theme be?

It started with the invite.

Tevin Campbell's save-the-date was pure drama: a phoenix in flames, gold letters, and one line, "Tevin Campbell's 50th Birthday, November 14, 2026, Los Angeles, CA."

## The party lived up to it

On Saturday night, a full house of friends, family and fans came out to celebrate in Los Angeles. Our sister outlet Newswire Hollywood was in the room, and the birthday boy did not just cut the cake. He took the microphone and sang for his guests.

## The glow-up is real

Campbell, born November 12, 1976, was a kid when Quincy Jones put him on the lead vocal of "Tomorrow" in 1989. Then came "Tell Me What You Want Me to Do," "Round and Round" and "Can We Talk," plus Grammy nominations and a run on Broadway in Hairspray.
