HEADLINE: Tevin Campbell Turns 50 and Gives God the Glory as Believers Pack His L.A. Birthday Celebration

DEK: The "Can We Talk" singer celebrated 50 years with friends, family and many fellow Christians, then picked up the mic and sang. Leave him a birthday blessing below.

BYLINE: Gospel News Access, with on-scene reporting by publisher Terry Bryant

He gave God the glory.

Tevin Campbell turned 50 this month, and on Saturday, November 14, 2026, he celebrated with a packed birthday party in Los Angeles. Many Christians were in the room to celebrate with him, according to Gospel News Access publisher Terry Bryant, who attended.

## "He thanked God"

Tevin loves God, and he personally thanked God for his birthday, Bryant said. It was a night of thanksgiving as much as a party: friends, family and fans gathered around a man whose voice has carried for nearly four decades.

Then the birthday boy picked up a microphone and sang for his guests.

## A gift from the start

Campbell's gift showed up early. He was still a child when Quincy Jones gave him the lead vocal on "Tomorrow (A Better You, Better Me)" on the 1989 album Back on the Block (source: Walt Disney Records biography, https://disney.go.com/disneyrecords/biographies/campbell_bio.html; BlackPast, https://www.blackpast.org/african-american-history/people-african-american-history/tevin-campbell-1976/).

The hits followed: "Tell Me What You Want Me to Do," "Round and Round" (written for him by Prince) and "Can We Talk," which earned him a Grammy nomination for Best Male R&B Vocal Performance. He was born November 12, 1976, in Waxahachie, Texas.
