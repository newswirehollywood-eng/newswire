## PART 1 — THE STRATEGY IN FIVE MOVES
How the top firms (42West, PMK•BNC, ID, Sunshine Sachs) would run tonight, adapted to us.

1. **ONE SOURCE, ONE VOICE.** Our press room page is the only place the facts live. PR STARPOWER is the only press contact. This is how we cover for a disorganized client: nobody sees the chaos, because every road leads to one clean page and one calm voice.
2. **LEAD WITH THE BIGGEST NAME.** Richard T. Jones (host) leads every headline, caption and pitch. DeVon Franklin is second, Nick Vallelonga (Oscar) third. Editors say yes to names they know.
3. **A DIFFERENT ANGLE FOR EVERY DESK.** Faith media: DeVon Franklin and stories of hope. Black press: Jones, Franklin, McKinney. Trades: the awards and 950 films. International: 30 nations. Local TV: celebrities in Beverly Hills tonight. Same event, five stories, five times the coverage.
4. **OWN THE PICTURES.** Most coverage of a small festival is the photos. Our team shoots, captions and publishes first, so outlets use ours with credit. Whoever owns the pictures owns the story.
5. **WIN THE MORNING AFTER.** Results go out the same night. The winners release lands before the trades' morning meeting. Speed is how a small festival looks big.

## PART 7 — DAMAGE CONTROL
- **Conflicting dates and venues online.** Our page is the single source; pitches link only there.
- **Old publicists named elsewhere.** If a reporter has an old contact, reply warmly and redirect to PR STARPOWER.
- **Talent no-show.** The page says "scheduled to appear" (industry standard). Never apologize in public; lead with who did come.
- **Claims we can't confirm** (for example, a flyer's "Grammy-winning"). We print what we can stand behind.
- **Something goes wrong on the night.** One statement, from Terry only, within 30 minutes: what happened, what we're doing, nothing more.
