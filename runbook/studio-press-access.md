## THE THING NOBODY TELLS YOU

There is no secret. The trades are not doing something clever that we cannot
copy. **Every major studio and streamer runs a press portal, and getting on it
is an application, not a relationship.**

Disney states the bar plainly:

> Freelance journalists must provide copies of current articles published in the
> past 6 months with the requester's byline and publication dateline, or provide
> a letter of assignment from the editor of an established publication.

That is the gate at essentially all of them. Published clips, a real byline, a
real dateline, on a real domain.

**We now clear that bar.** Nine published stories, desk bylines, datelines, a
live domain, a masthead, a standards page and a press room. Three weeks ago we
did not. This is the moment the applications become winnable.

### What access actually gets us

- **Press releases before they are public** — the announcement hits our inbox at
  embargo time, not after Deadline has posted it. This is the single biggest
  speed advantage in the business.
- **Licensed key art and stills** — solves the photo problem permanently for
  every title we cover. Press-kit images are licensed for editorial use by
  outlets covering that title.
- **Screeners** — reviews published on release day rather than three days later.
- **Junkets, premieres, set visits** — the invitations flow from being on the
  list, not from asking each time.
- **Named publicist contacts** — a human to call on a breaking story instead of
  a general inbox.

---

## IMPORTANT: most of these are FORMS, not emails

The instinct is to draft an email. For most of these, that is the slower path
and it can get you ignored. **Registering on the portal is the primary route;
the email is the follow-up when a form is rejected, or when there is no form.**

Do the forms first. They are quick and they create a record in their system.

## THE BLOCKER NOBODY WANTS TO HEAR

**A verified press account is worthless if nobody reads the inbox.**

Every one of these portals verifies by email. Every embargoed release, screener
link, junket invitation and RSVP deadline arrives by email, most with a deadline
attached. Terry has said he is not currently checking email.

If we apply to twelve studios and the replies sit unread, we will have spent the
effort and gained nothing — and worse, a missed junket RSVP or a broken embargo
gets an outlet quietly dropped from a list, which is far harder to undo than
never being on it.

**info@newswirehollywood.com exists** — GoDaddy-hosted on Microsoft 365, already
paid for (see runbook/services.md). It needs to be somewhere Terry actually
looks, daily. Options, easiest first:

1. **Forward it to the phone.** Add the account to the iPhone Mail app, or
   forward info@ to whatever address Terry genuinely reads. Ten minutes, zero
   cost, solves it today.
2. **Shared inbox with a second reader.** Someone on the team also watches it,
   so nothing dies while Terry is on a carpet.
3. **The Email Triage agent** (`.claude/agents/email-triage-agent.md`) — already
   written, blocked on mailbox API access. Once connected it can sort, flag
   embargoes and deadlines, and draft routine replies. Built for exactly this.

Nothing else on this page matters until one of those three is true.

## SEQUENCE

1. Sort the inbox. Non-negotiable first step.
2. Register on the Tier 1 portals — the forms, not emails. One sitting.
3. Send the email below only where there is no form, or a form was rejected.
4. Log every application in `data/contacts.md` with the date, so follow-ups are
   possible and nobody is pitched twice.
5. Follow up once after 10 business days. Once. Never twice.
6. After the first three stories published with their material, ask the named
   publicist for the junket and premiere list. That is the upgrade from
   list-member to invited.

## ON "DOMINATING"

Worth saying plainly, because it shapes what works.

These are not adversaries to beat. Studio publicists are the people whose job is
to get their title covered. **They want more legitimate outlets, not fewer** — a
new credible LA outlet with a working site and a fast publishing pipeline is
useful to them.

We do not win by outmanoeuvring them. We win by being the outlet that files
accurately, respects embargoes without exception, credits correctly, and
publishes within minutes instead of hours. Those four things are what moves an
outlet from the general list to the phone call before the announcement.

The embargo point is the one that cannot be got wrong. One broken embargo ends
the relationship permanently, across the whole studio, and the other studios
hear about it. Our publishing engine carries an embargo field for exactly this
reason.
