## 4. ENGINE ONE: BE FOUND EVERYWHERE NEWS IS READ

Ranking now happens in four places, not one. We have to win all four.

### a) Google Search and Top Stories
- **Search Console for all three sites.** Terry adds and verifies each site. This
  is how we see what ranks, in which country, and fix what doesn't.
- **Google News inclusion is automatic now.** Google stopped using manual Publisher
  Center submissions in 2025. Sites that follow its content policies are considered
  automatically. Our job is the signals: speed, original reporting, real bylines and
  clear dates.
- **Real names on stories.** Google rewards visible expertise and trust. Desk
  bylines ("By the Music Desk") are safe, but a named editor ranks better.
  Recommendation: "By Terry Bryant, Senior Editor" on our biggest pieces, with an
  author page listing his credentials.

### b) Google Discover, the biggest gap we found
Discover is the news feed inside every Android phone and the Google app, worldwide.
It is the number one Google traffic source for most news publishers. It shows large
photos only when a site gives permission with one setting, and **none of our sites
has it yet.**
- Fix: add the large-image setting to every page, and make sure each lead photo is
  at least 1200 pixels wide. This is the system's job; ten minutes per site.

### c) AI answer engines: the new front page
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Mode and Microsoft Copilot now answer entertainment
questions directly. Being the source they cite is the new page one.
- **ChatGPT search:** make sure OpenAI's search crawler (OAI-SearchBot) is allowed in
  each site's robots file. A site that blocks it does not appear in ChatGPT's cited
  answers.
- **Perplexity Publisher Program:** Perplexity pays enrolled publishers a share of
  revenue when it cites them. Apply for all three sites. Free money for content we
  already publish.
- **Write for citation.** AI engines quote clean facts: who, what, when, where, in a
  box. The fact box on the Freda Payne story is exactly the format they lift. Every
  event story gets one.
- **Training crawlers are a separate decision.** Crawlers that train AI models (not
  search) are worth leaving blocked or charging for. Big publishers are being paid
  licensing fees for their archives. Our archive becomes an asset; we don't give it
  away by default.
- **An llms.txt file** on each site: a plain list that tells AI engines what we are
  and where our best work is. New, cheap, worth doing.

### d) The news apps and aggregators
| Outlet | What it reaches | How |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Start / MSN | Huge in the UK, Europe and every Windows PC | Partner application |
| Apple News | iPhone users worldwide | Apple News publisher application |
| Flipboard, SmartNews, NewsBreak | Mobile news readers | Feed submission (our RSS is ready) |
| Bing (powers Copilot, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo) | Search beyond Google | Bing Webmaster Tools; IndexNow already pings it |

### e) Social, by region, because each region lives on a different app
| Region | Where people get news |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia and the Gulf | **Snapchat** (enormous in Saudi), X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube |
| Middle East, Africa, Latin America, India | **WhatsApp Channels**: free broadcast to unlimited followers |
| UK and Europe | Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads |
| Asia | YouTube, Instagram; LINE in Japan later |
| Everywhere | YouTube Shorts and TikTok for red-carpet clips |

Every story gets a vertical video cut and a WhatsApp Channel post. That is how one
story reaches five regions in one hour.

## 5. ENGINE TWO: THE MIDDLE EAST AND THE WORLD

### Why the Middle East now
Saudi Arabia and the UAE are spending more on entertainment than any region on Earth:
Riyadh Season, the Joy Awards, MDLBEAST, Red Sea, GITEX, LEAP, FII and Dubai Fashion
Week. Hollywood stars fly in every month. Few American outlets cover it well.

### What we build
1. **A "Global Spotlight" section** on Newswire Hollywood and Hollywood News Access:
   Middle East, Asia (K-pop, Bollywood, Japan), Africa (Nollywood, Afrobeats), Latin
   and Europe.
2. **An Arabic edition, then Spanish, then Portuguese.** Each one is properly linked
   so Google shows the right language in each country. Translation can be
   AI-assisted, but a named human signs off on every story; that rule doesn't
   change.
3. **Stringers:** paid-per-story local contributors in Dubai, Riyadh, Cairo, Lagos,
   London and Seoul, credited by name.
4. **Media partnerships with festivals.** Festivals give accreditation, logo
   placement and access to outlets that sign on as media partners. Start with El
   Gouna, Cairo, Marrakech, Doha and Red Sea (2027).

### The rule we must respect: licensing
- **UAE:** since May 2025, anyone doing media activity or paid content in the UAE
  needs a business license plus a media permit from the UAE Media Council. Fines run
  from AED 10,000 to AED 1,000,000.
- **Saudi Arabia** has its own media licensing.
- Reporting on the region from Los Angeles is ordinary journalism. But before anyone
  works on the ground for us in Dubai or Riyadh, or we sell sponsored content there,
  we check the license with a local adviser. That one check protects the whole
  operation.

## 6. ENGINE THREE: THE GATEWAY TO HOLLYWOOD

Terry's idea: much of the world's talent can't get into Hollywood. We become its
front door.

- **Global Voices:** a standing submission lane in the press room where international
  artists, films and festivals send their news. Editorial picks run free. Paid
  placements run through the PAID OUT lane, labeled Sponsored.
- **The Global Spotlight List:** an annual honors list, "50 International Names
  Hollywood Needs to Know." It costs nothing, gets shared in every country on the
  list, and builds the brand that a live awards show will later stand on.
- **Our own festival and awards (future).** Before any event:
  1. trademark the name;
  2. build the list two years running;
  3. partner with an existing venue and festival for year one.

  That is how the Joy Awards, Red Sea and Critics Choice each grew.
- **PR STARPOWER becomes the international publicity desk.** Overseas publicists and
  government film commissions pay American PR firms to place their talent in
  Hollywood. That is a real market, and we sit right on top of it.

## 7. ENGINE FOUR: BE THE SOURCE

Dates are public. Contacts are not. Getting the contact is the job.

1. **The calendar** (section 3) tells us where to be.
2. **The Rolodex** (private, in Terry's Google Drive) tells us who to call: press
   offices, publicists, sponsors, gift-suite producers, venue contacts and festival
   accreditation desks. It can grow to 20,000 entries, with a PDF snapshot every
   month or two.
3. **Gatekeepers per event:** for each big event we log the organizer's press office,
   the PR agency of record, the sponsors (sponsors' PR teams run their own parties and
   suites), the gift-suite producers, and the after-party hosts. These are the back
   doors: a sponsor's PR agency can credential you to its party even when the main
   event is full.
4. **Gift suites and lounges:** known operators include Distinctive Assets (Emmys,
   Oscars), GBK Brand Bar (Globes, Grammys, Oscars) and DPA (Globes week). They want
   press; press coverage is what brands pay them for.
5. **Credentials need clips, and we have them.** Studios and awards bodies ask for
   bylined, dated articles from the last six months. We now have them across three
   sites.
6. **Monitor the chatter.** The trend-watch agent scans what's breaking on social and
   blogs. We confirm before we publish. A tip is a lead, not a story.

## 10. GUARDRAILS THAT PROTECT THE RANKINGS

Domination is won on speed and lost on one bad story.
- **Accuracy:** we confirm before we publish. Google, Apple and MSN all down-rank
  outlets with repeated errors.
- **Mass AI-written pages get penalized.** Google's spam rules punish content
  produced at scale with no original value. AI helps us go faster, but every story
  has original reporting, our photos or our access.
- **Embargoes are absolute.** One break ends a studio relationship permanently.
- **Labels on every paid piece.**
- **Corrections in the open,** dated on the page.
