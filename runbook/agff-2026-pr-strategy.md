## 3. THE FREE BLAST — ROUTES THAT COST NOTHING
In order of impact for tonight.

1. **Direct email to assignment desks** (media list). TV desks plan crews in the morning meeting, 8–9 AM. Send the media alert tonight, resend at 7 AM with "TONIGHT" in the subject line, then call the desk at 9 AM. The call is what gets the crew.
2. **City News Service** (news@socalnews.com). Their daybook lands on every LA newsroom desk. One email reaches dozens of outlets.
3. **Our own wires**: Newswire Hollywood (live), Hollywood News Access and Gospel News Access. Same facts, different angles, so Google treats each as its own story.
4. **Social, all accounts**: Newswire Hollywood auto-poster (running), PR STARPOWER, every nominee, every volunteer. Instagram **Collab posts** put one post on two accounts at once, so ask the festival account to accept collabs.
5. **Free release sites**: PRLog, PR.com (free tier), OpenPR. Low prestige, but they get indexed by Google within hours and show up in AI search answers.
6. **Free community calendars**: Patch (Beverly Hills), LAist "Best Things To Do", Beverly Press calendar, Eventbrite (already listed), Facebook Event.
7. **Talent's own channels**: ask Richard T. Jones' and DeVon Franklin's teams for one story post each. One post from a known name beats fifty emails.

## 4. THE TECHNOLOGY EDGE — WHAT THE PROS USE NOW
- **Event schema** (done): our page tells Google it is an event with a date and venue, so it can show in Google's event results.

- **AI search** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI answers): these read press rooms and release sites. A clean page with dates, names and venue gets quoted. Ours is built that way.

- **Instagram Collab and Broadcast Channels; WhatsApp Channels**: one tap reaches every follower.

## 6. DAMAGE CONTROL — COVERING FOR A SMALL, DISORGANIZED CLIENT
- **Conflicting dates online** (June, September, November; Writers Guild vs. Landmark): our press room is now the single source. Every pitch links there. Ask the festival to point its own site there.
- **Two publicists named** (Em Fergusson, April): the release names PR STARPOWER as the media contact. If a reporter cites old contacts, reply warmly and redirect.
- **Unverified claims on flyers** (for example, "Grammy-winning"): we print only what we can stand behind. Accuracy is the brand.
- **Talent no-shows**: the page says "scheduled to appear," which is the industry-standard language that protects us.
