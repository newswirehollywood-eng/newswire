## 3. Getting paid for the music
1. Distribution (TuneCore): turn on Facebook/Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Content ID. The songs then appear in those apps' music libraries; Terry earns when anyone uses them.
2. Songwriter royalties: register every song with a PRO (BMI is free for songwriters) and a publishing administrator (TuneCore Publishing or Songtrust) to collect performance and mechanical royalties, including social and streaming plays.
3. SoundExchange: register as the master owner for digital radio royalties.
4. Sync licensing: license songs to filmmakers and TV. Start with AGFF's filmmakers (950 films). A fee per film, plus credits.
5. Keep each song in one lane: a song in Content ID should not also be sold as "royalty-free", or buyers' videos get claimed.
