## 3. Our own wire service

Yes, it is possible, and Newswire Hollywood was designed for it (the PAID OUT intake path in CLAUDE.md).

What we can run ourselves, at near-zero cost:
- Release pages on our four sites, each with NewsArticle data Google reads
- RSS feeds newsrooms and aggregators can subscribe to
- Inclusion in Google News, Apple News and Bing (section 1)
- Email to an opt-in journalist list (needs a sending service; the Rolodex stays private)
- Social blast (section 2), with agents writing every version

What paid wires have that we cannot copy quickly:
- Contracted placement on hundreds of partner sites (Yahoo Finance, AP-linked and local news sites)
- Feeds into Bloomberg, Factiva and newsroom systems
- Large verified journalist databases

Rules if we sell it:
- Every paid release carries a visible "Press release" or "Sponsored" label
- Every link inside a paid release carries rel="sponsored". Paid links without it break Google's rules and can sink the whole site's rankings
- Never promise guaranteed media pickup
- Revenue does not direct newsroom coverage. Where they meet, disclose

Hybrid until our wire has reach: publish on our network, and for a big client blast add one budget paid wire. Third-party reviews put EIN Presswire at about $149 for a single release and Send2Press from about $99 (state) or $199 (national). Confirm on the vendor's page before paying.
