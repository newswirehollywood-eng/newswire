# Getting into the news apps and AI engines — application guide

Checked October 8, 2026. These programs change often; each step says what is confirmed and what is not.
Applies to: Newswire Hollywood, Hollywood News Access, Gospel News Access. (HTA is a service business, not a news brand: skip AdSense there.)

## Before any application (already done by Claude)
- Large images allowed for Google Discover (`max-image-preview:large`) on every page.
- AI search engines explicitly welcomed in robots.txt (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude).
- `llms.txt` on every site: a plain map of the site for AI engines.
- "At a glance" fact boxes on event stories.
- Privacy, about and contact pages exist (AdSense and Apple both check).

## 1. Google News — nothing to apply for
Google now includes eligible news sites automatically. What helps: Search Console verified, news sitemap submitted, real bylines and dates. 
Terry: add each site to https://search.google.com/search-console and submit the sitemap.

## 2. Bing / MSN / Copilot
- Bing Webmaster Tools: https://www.bing.com/webmasters → "Import from Google Search Console". Free, 2 minutes. Feeds Bing, Copilot and DuckDuckGo.
- MSN Partner Hub (articles shown on MSN.com): a **limited pilot**. Apply through the application linked from the official MSN Partner Hub plugin page: https://wordpress.org/plugins/microsoft-start/ . MSN contacts publishers it accepts. Judged on original reporting, attribution and editorial standards.

## 3. Apple News
- Sign up at https://www.icloud.com/newspublisher/ with an Apple ID (Terry's), accept the agreement, add the channel (logo PNG at least 256×256, under 2 MB) and the RSS feed.
- Caution: in 2024–25 Apple told some publishers it was not accepting unsolicited applications. Apply anyway; it costs nothing. If refused, we reapply once traffic grows.
- Feeds ready: newswirehollywood.com/feed.xml, hollywoodnewsaccess.com/feed.xml, gospelnewsaccess.com/feed.xml

## 4. Perplexity
- The Publisher Program is **by invitation/partnership**, not an open form. Our part: stay crawlable (done) and be cited. Once a site has steady traffic, Claude drafts a partnership pitch for Terry to send through Perplexity's official contact page (perplexity.ai).

## 5. Google AdSense (revenue)
- Apply at https://adsense.google.com with Terry's Google account (must be 18+).
- Google's stated bar: original content that meets its policies. No official minimum traffic or post count, but thin sites are rejected as "low value content".
- Recommended order: Newswire first (most original stories), then Gospel, then HNA.
- Rule: paid/sponsored content stays labeled; ads never sit inside the article text of a story about an advertiser.
