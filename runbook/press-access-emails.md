# PRESS ACCESS — COPY-PASTE EMAILS

Drafted 2026-09-27. Use these where a studio has no registration form, or where
a form application was rejected or went unanswered.

Register on the portal FIRST where one exists — see `studio-press-access.md`.
A form application creates a record in their system; a cold email may not.

**Send from info@newswirehollywood.com.** Not a personal address. A studio
publicity desk checks the domain against the outlet, and a gmail address from a
publication that owns a domain reads as a freelancer or a fan.

## RULES FOR ALL OF THEM

**Never claim staff, circulation figures or traffic we cannot evidence.** A
publicity desk can check, and an outlet caught inflating itself is done. What we
have is genuinely enough: a live site, daily publishing, real bylines, a
standards page, credentials.

**Never send the same email twice to the same desk.** Log every send in
`data/contacts.md` with the date.

**Honour every embargo, without exception.** One break ends the relationship
across the entire studio, permanently, and the other studios hear about it.

**Reply within the day when they answer.** The whole point of being on a list is
speed. A desk that waits two days for a reply stops sending.

**Credit every supplied image to its source, every time.** It is the licence
term, not a courtesy.
