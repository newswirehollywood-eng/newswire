## Set up a Project (one time each)
1. Open https://claude.ai/projects and click **Create project**.
2. Name it, then paste the matching instructions below into **Project instructions**.
3. Add files under **Project knowledge** (the NDA template, rate sheets, client briefs).

## Vetting checklist (insurance agents, vendors, partners)
1. License: California Department of Insurance "Check a License" (https://interactive.web.insurance.ca.gov/), New York DFS for NY, NIPR for other states. Note license number, status, lines of authority.
2. Discipline and lawsuits: same license page, plus the LA Superior Court case search and BBB.
3. Carriers: which insurers appoint them.
4. Errors & omissions insurance: ask for the certificate.
5. Two references from high-net-worth or entertainment clients.
6. NDA signed before any client or talent name is shared.
7. Compensation in writing. Insurance referral fees have special state rules: have counsel confirm before agreeing to any split.
