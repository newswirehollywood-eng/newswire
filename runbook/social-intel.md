# SOCIAL INTEL: WHERE THE TIPS LIVE

Terry's instruction, 2026-10-05: have a hand on the pulse of what is being said on
Instagram, TikTok and the blogs, because the people closest to the stars
(stylists, hair and makeup artists, party photographers, bloggers) often know first.

This list is public accounts only. Named private sources go in the private Rolodex
in Terry's Google Drive, never here.

## THE RULE THAT KEEPS US CREDIBLE
Everything from social media is a LEAD, not a story. We confirm with the rep, the
venue, the organizer or a second independent source before we publish. Blind items
are never printed. Photos from someone's Instagram are not ours: we link or use the
platform's official embed, or license the image (see the image rule in CLAUDE.md).

## HOW WE WORK IT
1. A newsroom "listening" account on Instagram, TikTok and X that follows this list,
   with post notifications on for the top 25.
2. Two sweeps a day, and a third on event nights from 5 to 9 p.m. Pacific.
3. Free Google Alerts on: "after party", "private dinner", "hosted by",
   "pre-Grammy", "gift suite", "brand activation", plus each A-list name we cover.
4. Relationships: glam teams and party photographers want credit and coverage.
   Offer both. Never ask anyone to break an NDA or share something private.
5. Instagram and TikTok block automated collection, and scraping breaks their
   terms. The system covers the open web and X through search; a person covers
   the apps.
