**Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic — a new, real AI-copyright fight, filed since our last sweep.** On August 28, 2026, Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed a federal copyright suit against Anthropic (and, by name, CEO Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann) in the Northern District of California, alleging Anthropic trained Claude on tens of thousands of song lyrics obtained via piracy sites and scraped from licensed lyric platforms like Musixmatch and LyricFind. They're seeking up to $150,000 per infringed work plus damages for removed copyright-management information — potentially billions. Anthropic has said it disagrees and will defend the case, arguing fair use. This is a filed lawsuit, a real and verifiable legal action, not a rumor or a settled outcome — the case is pending, no ruling yet. Why it matters to Hollywood specifically: it's the music-publishing counterpart to the studio-side AI-training fights already playing out, and a data point for how aggressively rightsholders are moving against AI labs training on entertainment IP. Sources: [Variety — Sony, Warner Sue Anthropic for Allegedly Illegally Training Claude](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/sony-music-publishing-warner-chappell-anthropic-lawsuit-1236847442/), [TechCrunch — Sony Music, Warner sue Anthropic, alleging a 'brazen campaign' of intellectual property theft](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic-alleging-a-brazen-campaign-of-intellectual-property-theft/).
