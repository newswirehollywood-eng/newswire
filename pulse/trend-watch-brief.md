**AI "actress" Tilly Norwood moves from stunt to announced feature film — still nothing you can watch yet.** Xicoia (the AI arm of production company Particle6) has been building out a synthetic performer, Tilly Norwood, since 2025 via shorts and a music video that do already exist and can be watched — that part is bucket 1, shipped and usable. What's new and still speculative: in July 2026 the company announced a first feature film, "Misaligned," built around the character. Why it matters: this is the clearest live test case of the AI-performer fight the union just finished bargaining over (see next item), and it's a real point of industry tension, not manufactured hype. **OpenAI's Sora-Disney deal is a case study in why we don't report "announced" as "available."** Disney and OpenAI signed a headline licensing deal (reported value: Disney taking a $1 billion stake in OpenAI) to bring more than 200 Disney/Marvel/Pixar/Star Wars characters into Sora for fan-made short videos, with curated clips planned for Disney+. That was bucket 3 (announced) at signing, briefly bucket 2 when the feature actually went live for some users. Then OpenAI shut down the Sora app and web product entirely in April 2026, months after the Disney deal was announced and before any money had actually changed hands — so the whole arc collapsed back to nothing usable. Why it matters to Hollywood specifically: it's a live example of how fast AI-video deals with studios can be announced, hyped, and unwound, which is exactly the pattern this desk exists to track skeptically rather than amplify. Sources: [Variety — OpenAI shutting down Sora video, Disney drops $1B investment plan](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/openai-shutting-down-sora-video-disney-1236698277/), [The Hollywood Reporter — Disney to invest $1B in OpenAI, boosting Sora in Hollywood](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/disney-to-invest-1-billion-in-openai-in-major-deal-that-boosts-sora-1236447942/), [OpenAI — Disney-Sora agreement announcement](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/).

**SAG-AFTRA's new AI contract terms are ratified and in effect — bucket 1, not a proposal anymore.** Members voted to ratify the union's four-year deal with the AMPTP (91.4% in favor, results announced June 4, 2026), which includes real, currently-binding AI provisions: producers can only use an AI performer instead of a live actor if it brings "significant additional value," plus digital-replica consent, notice-and-bargain rights if studios move toward synthetic performers, and a pension-fund merger set for January 2028. This is genuinely shipped — it's the operative contract governing AI use on union productions right now, not a pending proposal. Why it matters: it's the actual rulebook Tilly Norwood-style projects have to operate around if they want union talent or union productions involved.
