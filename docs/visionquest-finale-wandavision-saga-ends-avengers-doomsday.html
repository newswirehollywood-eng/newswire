VisionQuest Finale Ends Marvel's WandaVision Saga With Two Post-Credits Scenes Aimed at Avengers: Doomsday

Paul Bettany's Vision got his family back, and Marvel tucked two codas into the last hour that point straight at December 18. Is Wanda really coming home?

By the Television Desk, Newswire Hollywood  |  Nov 11, 2026  |  Los Angeles

Three shows. Five years. One very complicated family. It is over.

Spoiler warning: this story discusses the ending of VisionQuest.

The series finale of Marvel's VisionQuest, titled "Forever," is now streaming on Disney+, and it closes what Marvel calls the WandaVision Saga: WandaVision in 2021, Agatha All Along in 2024, and now Vision's own show, Variety reported.

The reunion

Paul Bettany's Vision ends the series reunited with his and Wanda's grown sons, Tommy and Billy Maximoff. Bettany, Ruaridh Mollica and showrunner Terry Matalas walked TheWrap through the late arrival that completes the family, including a scene that was cut.

Bettany celebrated the ending in New York with co-star James Spader, Laughing Place reported.

Two codas, one target

The finale carries two post-credits scenes. Variety reads them as a tease that Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch is coming back, with Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom in the frame. GamesRadar says the second scene is footage from Avengers: Doomsday itself.

Matalas talked through his thinking on Wanda with TheWrap. Marvel has not officially named Bettany in the Doomsday cast, though Anthony Mackie has said he spent time with Bettany during filming, Screen Rant noted.

Why it matters

Disney+ needs Marvel TV that moves the movies, and this is the cleanest handoff the studio has pulled off in years: a streaming finale that works as a trailer for its biggest film of the season. Avengers: Doomsday opens December 18, five weeks from now.

Did the finale earn the reunion, or was it all about the codas?
