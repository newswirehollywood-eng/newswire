Freda Payne’s “Legendary Ladies of Song” plays to a sold-out house at Catalina Jazz Club

Don B. Welch presents the legendary Freda Payne in a sold-out tribute to nine women who built American jazz and pop, staged on Sunset Boulevard on VMAs weekend.

LOS ANGELES — Freda Payne Portrays and Honors Legendary Ladies of Song, written and directed by Donald B. Welch and presented by Don B. Welch, played to a sold-out house at Catalina Jazz Club, 6725 Sunset Boulevard, on Saturday, September 26, 2026 at 8:30 p.m.

The production stars Freda Payne in tribute to nine artists who shaped American song: Ella Fitzgerald, Sarah Vaughan, Peggy Lee, Gloria Lynne, Dakota Staton, Lena Horne, Nancy Wilson, Dinah Washington and Eartha Kitt. Payne knew or met several of them personally, and the evening drew on that first-hand history as well as the songbook.

Payne’s programme included “How High the Moon” (Ella Fitzgerald), “I Wish You Love” (Gloria Lynne), “The Late Late Show” (Dakota Staton), “Body and Soul” (Sarah Vaughan) and “Guess Who I Saw Today” (Nancy Wilson).

Cast and company

Special guest Thomasina Gross opened the show portraying Eartha Kitt. The evening was announced by Gregory Abbott Jr. Actors Terryl DaLuz and Kyrel Jaundoo appeared, with Chuka Aruh and Mi-cha-El Wat as escorts.

The band was led by musical director and pianist Josh Nelson, with Chris Colangelo on bass, Ricky Woodard on saxophone and Marcello Carelli on drums.

In the house

Loretta Devine and Stefanie Powers were among the audience. The performance fell on the same weekend as the MTV Video Music Awards in downtown Los Angeles.

Quotes

“The idea of doing a show honouring the legendary ladies of song and jazz was brought to me by my dear friend Donald B. Welch,” said Payne. “All these ladies were some of my favourite singers back when I started wanting to be a professional singer.”

On Thomasina Gross as Eartha Kitt: “Just to see her physically, she looks like Eartha Kitt — and she does this portrayal spot on.”

On musical director Josh Nelson: “There are a lot of good pianists, but Josh Nelson is one of the best.”

About Freda Payne

Freda Payne is a singer and actress whose career spans six decades, best known internationally for “Band of Gold.” Her memoir, Band of Gold: A Memoir, is available now. She celebrated her birthday on September 19, one week before the Catalina performance.

Upcoming

Payne performs aboard Holland America at the end of October 2026, sailing from Fort Lauderdale, Florida to Puerto Rico and returning, on a bill that also includes Darlene Love and Wayne Newton.
