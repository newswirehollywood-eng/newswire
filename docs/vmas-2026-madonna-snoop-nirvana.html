VMAs return to Los Angeles Sunday: Madonna opens after 23 years, Snoop Dogg hosts, Nirvana takes the Vanguard

The 2026 MTV Video Music Awards air live from Los Angeles on Sunday. Madonna leads all nominees with thirteen and opens the show. Taylor Swift receives a newly created honor, and premieres a video during the broadcast.

The 2026 MTV Video Music Awards air live from Los Angeles on Sunday, September 27, from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m. Eastern — 4:30 to 6:30 Pacific — on CBS, simulcast on MTV and streaming on Paramount+.

Snoop Dogg hosts. Madonna opens the show, her first VMAs performance in twenty-three years, and arrives as the night's most-nominated artist with thirteen.

Those two facts sit oddly well together. The VMAs spent the last decade being written about as a show in search of a reason to exist, and the fix, this year, is not a new format. It is the two performers most associated with the ceremony's own mythology, back on the stage that made them.

Nirvana receives the Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award. Taylor Swift takes the inaugural MTV VMA Artist Director Honors, a category created this year, and the video for her single “Patient Zero” premieres during the broadcast.

The performance roster runs to Raye, Sienna Spiro, Lisa of Blackpink, Gunna, Shaboozey and GENER8ION. Kacey Musgraves performs a tribute to Dolly Parton. Raye, sombr and Teddy Swims honour George Michael.

Two tributes and a posthumous Vanguard is a lot of looking backward for a show built on what is next. Whether that reads as reverence or as hedging is the question Sunday actually answers.
