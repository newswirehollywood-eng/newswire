Newswire Hollywood and Hollywood News Access to Cover SKVLK Fest 2026 in Los Angeles

The entertainment outlets will report live from Saturday’s youth-culture music and carnival festival alongside Billboard and other major media, including the Dolls Kill-styled VIP Creator Lounge.

Video: the SKVLK Fest 2026 announcement reel will be embedded here when it is published.

LOS ANGELES (Nov. 6, 2026) — Newswire Hollywood and Hollywood News Access will be on the ground Saturday, Nov. 7, to cover SKVLK Fest 2026, the Los Angeles youth-culture music and carnival festival that drew 25,000 guests last year and turned away 15,000 more at capacity, according to the festival.

The two outlets join Billboard and other major media at the festival, with arrivals, performances, red-carpet interviews and behind-the-scenes coverage across their websites and their Facebook and Instagram accounts through the night.

Coverage will include live reports from the festival’s VIP Creator Lounge, produced by PR STARPOWER and styled by sponsor Dolls Kill, where a hand-picked group of creators will document the night for their own audiences.

SKVLK Fest was founded by Anelise, the daughter of Academy Award winner Jamie Foxx. The 2025 edition featured performances by GloRilla, Tyga, Leon Thomas and YK Osiris, according to published reports, and reached more than 100 million people, according to festival figures. Organizers say this year’s edition is bigger.

Stories, photos and video from the festival will run on newswirehollywood.com and hollywoodnewsaccess.com, and will be available to partner outlets through the Newswire Hollywood wire.
