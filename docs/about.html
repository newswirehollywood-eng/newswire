About us

Giving the world a voice

World Newswire Press is a global newsroom and wire service. We report the world from every region, carry the voices too often left out, and make it simple for anyone, anywhere, to bring a story to an editor.

What we do

  • Original reporting from our World Desk and correspondents, across the Americas, Europe, Africa, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific, plus business, entertainment, and faith and culture.
  • The Wire: live headlines from more than thirty of the world’s newsrooms, each credited to the outlet that published it and linked to its story. We never republish their articles.
  • The Press Room: news release distribution for organizations worldwide, always labeled.

Our standards

  • Named sign-off. Nothing we originate is published without the approval of a named editor, and we record who approved it.
  • Sources shown. Our stories list the sources they rely on, with links. We write in our own words and never copy other outlets’ sentences.
  • Confirmed facts only. If something cannot be confirmed, we leave it out or say plainly that it is unconfirmed.
  • Licensed images only. Every photo we publish is our own, licensed, public domain, or supplied with written permission, and we credit it.
  • Embargoes are hard stops. An embargoed story does not appear anywhere on the site, in our feed or in our sitemaps until its time.
  • Labels you can see. Sponsored content and press releases are always labeled. Business relationships never direct our coverage, and we disclose them when they touch a story.
  • Protecting sources. We never reveal a confidential source without their permission.

Corrections

We fix mistakes quickly and in the open. If you find an error, email info@worldnewswirepress.com with the link and what is wrong. An editor will review it, correct the story if we got it wrong, and add a note at the end of the story saying what was changed and when.

Ownership

World Newswire Press is part of the Newswire Hollywood media group. Terry Bryant is the editor.
