The Wire

Content that reaches this site by a different path than our own reporting: coverage written elsewhere and carried with attribution, and paid releases formatted and distributed on behalf of a client. Both run here, clearly labeled, and neither is edited or selected by the Newsroom.

Why this page exists separately Newswire Hollywood moves content three ways: Originated (our own reporting), Syndicated In (written elsewhere, run here tagged and attributed), and Paid Out (a client pays, we format and distribute it, labeled as sponsored). The Wire is where the second and third live. Nothing on this page was assigned, reported, or edited by the Newsroom, and nothing on the Newsroom's own pages is paid placement. That separation is the whole point — see our editorial standards.

Syndicated

Written elsewhere, run here with full attribution to the outlet that reported it first.

Nothing has run through the Syndicated intake path yet. When it does, it appears here tagged Syndicated with the original outlet credited in the byline.

Sponsored

Paid distribution, formatted to house template, always labeled.

Nothing has run through the Paid intake path yet. Rate card and submission details are on the Press Room page. When a release runs, it appears here tagged Sponsored.
