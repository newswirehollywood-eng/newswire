Freda Payne Sings for Veterans at Motown Benefit Concert in Tempe Jan. 8

Fifty-five years after “Bring the Boys Home,” the “Band of Gold” legend heads to Tempe to sing for veterans on a bill with the Former Ladies of the Supremes and the Temptations Revue.

By the Music Desk, Newswire Hollywood · Nov. 19, 2026

LOS ANGELES — In 1971, Freda Payne sang “Bring the Boys Home,” a Vietnam-era plea to bring American soldiers home from the war. On Jan. 8, the “Band of Gold” singer sings for the veterans who made it back.

Payne, 84, is on the bill for the Motown for Veterans Benefit Concert at Mullett Arena in Tempe, Ariz., alongside the Former Ladies of the Supremes, Glenn Leonard’s Temptations Revue, Jr. Walker’s All-Star Band and Alli Starr. Doors open at 6 p.m.; the music starts at 7.

The money goes to Green Homes for Vets, a 501(c)(3) building an eco-friendly home community in Prescott Valley, Ariz., for combat service men and women returning from duty, according to the arena’s listing.

Detroit, by way of everywhere

The Motown banner fits the Detroit sound Payne grew up in, even though her biggest hits came out on Invictus, the label Holland-Dozier-Holland founded after leaving Motown. “Band of Gold” went to No. 1 in the United Kingdom in 1970 and made her a name worldwide.

She arrives in Tempe off a strong fall. In September she sold out Catalina Jazz Club in Hollywood with Legendary Ladies of Song, a night this desk reviewed as “not a tribute show” but “testimony.” In late October she performed at sea on a Holland America sailing with Darlene Love and Wayne Newton.

Getting in

Tickets are on Ticketmaster, listed from $89 to $235, with premium suites, loge upgrades, VIP meet-and-greet packages and an online silent auction also offered.
