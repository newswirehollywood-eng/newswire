The winners — updating

Confirmed so far, sourced and cited. This is not yet the complete list of all 26 main-ceremony categories — we're adding to it as more results are verified, not guessing at the rest to fill it out.

Comedy

Outstanding Comedy Series: "Hacks"

A win for the show's fifth and final season, which also set a record for the most nominations ever earned by a comedy in a single year (24). Jean Smart won Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series for the same show.

Sept 5–6

Creative Arts recap: a big night for "Widow's Bay" and "DTF"

Apple's "Widow's Bay" won guest acting (Betty Gilpin), casting, sound mixing, production design, cinematography, music supervision and composition, and sound editing. "DTF" swept directing and writing for creator Steven Conrad and won both limited-series supporting acting categories (David Harbour, Linda Cardellini), plus cinematography and picture editing.

Sourced via Deadline

What Emmy week actually looks like Three straight days of movement: high teas, charity luncheons, nominee toasts, gifting lounges, then the ceremony, the Governors Gala, and the studio parties that run past midnight. Talent moves between four and six events in a weekend, which is why credentialing for the smaller daytime events is often easier — and more productive — than chasing the ceremony carpet.

The red carpet: what actually happened

Monday's ceremony carpet, as reported by the fashion press — not a forecast anymore.

The looks that led coverage Zendaya wore a sheer white gown with silver beading, Mikimoto jewelry, and a new pixie cut. Chase Infiniti walked in a fully-beaded dark brown custom Louis Vuitton gown. Colman Domingo wore a custom Valentino ensemble, white slacks paired with a tan jacket. Keri Russell chose an all-black turtleneck leather gown with a low back; Supriya Ganesh wore an ice-blue silk gown with ruffle accents; Derek Hough arrived in an emerald green suit.
Confirmed

The archival-over-custom call held up

Our own forecast ahead of the ceremony pointed to archival and pre-existing runway pieces winning out over new custom commissions, given how tightly fashion month's New York-Milan-Paris run sits against the Emmys calendar. Monday's carpet bore that out — several of the night's most-discussed looks leaned archival rather than freshly built for the occasion.
