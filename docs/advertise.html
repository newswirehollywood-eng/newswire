Who you reach

Our readers follow Hollywood as a business. They are publicists, producers, talent and management teams, studio, streaming and label staff, event organizers, and engaged fans of film, television and music who want the story behind the headline. We are based in Los Angeles and cover the city’s premieres, red carpets and industry events in person.

  • On the site: original reporting, The Wire, the Hollywood Events Calendar and our Press Room.
  • On social: Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, where our stories and event coverage are pushed every day.
  • Across the network: package deals can run alongside Hollywood News Access (celebrity and pop culture), World Newswire Press (global news) and Gospel News Access (gospel and faith).

Audience figures available on request. We share current traffic and social numbers directly with advertisers rather than printing round numbers here, so what you see is current and verifiable.

How to book

  1. Tell us what you need. The format, your dates, and a link to what you are promoting.
  2. We confirm and invoice. We check the dates, confirm the package and price in writing, and send an invoice.
  3. We publish and report. Once the invoice is paid, your placement goes live on schedule and you receive the live links.

Our promise to readers and advertisers

  • Paid content is always labeled. Sponsored articles say “Sponsored”, banners say “Advertisement”, releases say “Press release”, and paid social posts carry #ad.
  • Advertisers never direct coverage. Buying an ad or a sponsored post does not buy, change or stop a news story. Our newsroom decides what it covers.
  • No ads inside paid stories. We do not place third-party ads inside the text of a sponsored article or a client’s press release.
  • Affiliate links are disclosed. Where we may earn a commission, the link is marked as sponsored and the page says so. See our privacy & ads policy.
