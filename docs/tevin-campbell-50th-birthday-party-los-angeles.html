Tevin Campbell Turns 50: The Quincy Jones Discovery Grabs the Mic at His Own Packed L.A. Party

The "Can We Talk" singer celebrated his 50th on Saturday in Los Angeles, and the birthday boy did not just blow out candles. He sang. Which Tevin song is your all-time favorite?

By the News Desk, Newswire Hollywood, with on-scene reporting by Terry Bryant  |  Nov 19, 2026  |  Los Angeles

Most people get a cake for their 50th. Tevin Campbell gave the room a show.

The R&B singer celebrated turning 50 on Saturday, November 14, 2026, with a packed birthday party in Los Angeles. A big crowd of friends, family and fans came out to celebrate, and at one point Campbell took the microphone and sang for his guests. Newswire Hollywood editor Terry Bryant was in the room.

The invitation set the tone: a save-the-date with a fiery phoenix rising behind gold letters reading "Tevin Campbell's 50th Birthday, November 14, 2026, Los Angeles, CA." Campbell's actual birthday is November 12; he was born in 1976 in Waxahachie, Texas, according to his Wikipedia biography.

Quincy Jones found him first

Campbell's story starts like a movie. He was still a kid when Quincy Jones put him on the lead vocal of "Tomorrow (A Better You, Better Me)" on Jones' 1989 album Back on the Block, according to his Walt Disney Records biography and BlackPast.

Then the hits came fast. Prince wrote and produced "Round and Round" for him. His debut album, T.E.V.I.N. (1991), gave R&B radio "Tell Me What You Want Me to Do." The 1993 album I'm Ready brought "Can We Talk," a Top 10 pop hit that earned him a Grammy nomination for Best Male R&B Vocal Performance. "Round and Round" was Grammy-nominated too.

Later he went to Broadway, starring in Hairspray, and in 2022 he spoke openly about his life and identity in an interview with People.

Why it matters

Nineties R&B is big business again. Brandy and Monica are taking "The Boy Is Mine" to a Las Vegas residency, and Usher and Chris Brown are filling stadiums. A full room on a Saturday night for Tevin Campbell is one more sign that the fans who grew up on these voices are still showing up.

What's next for Campbell? He has not announced new music or tour dates. When he does, we will have it.
