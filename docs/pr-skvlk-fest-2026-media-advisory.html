Media Advisory: Interviews With SKVLK Fest 2026 Creators, Producers and Partners Available Through PR STARPOWER

PR STARPOWER is fielding interview requests and press questions for Saturday’s SKVLK Fest in Los Angeles, the youth-culture music and carnival festival sponsored in its VIP Creator Lounge by Dolls Kill.

LOS ANGELES (Nov. 6, 2026) — PR STARPOWER, which leads sponsorship, brand partnerships and publicity for SKVLK Fest 2026, is taking interview requests and press inquiries for the festival, which takes place Saturday, Nov. 7, in Los Angeles.

Reporters, producers and bookers who want to speak with the festival’s creators, producers, sponsor or anyone connected to SKVLK Fest, or who have questions about coverage, should contact PR STARPOWER directly.

Media advisory
What
SKVLK Fest 2026, a Los Angeles youth-culture music and carnival festival founded by Anelise, the daughter of Academy Award winner Jamie Foxx. Its 2025 edition drew 25,000 guests and turned away 15,000 more at capacity, according to the festival.
When
Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026
Where
Los Angeles. The festival is held at a private location; details go to approved media only.
Interviews
Requests for the festival’s creators and producers, the PR STARPOWER lounge team, sponsor Dolls Kill and creators in the VIP Creator Lounge are coordinated by PR STARPOWER. Requests involving the Foxx family are passed along for consideration; availability is not guaranteed.
Visuals
Performances, a carnival setting, red-carpet arrivals and the Dolls Kill-styled VIP Creator Lounge.
Coverage on site
Newswire Hollywood, Hollywood News Access, and Billboard among other outlets
How to request
Email info@prstarpower.com with your name, outlet, role, the interview you want, your deadline and a link to recent work.

Guidelines for media

Many SKVLK Fest guests are under 18. Media may not photograph, film or interview minors without a parent’s permission. Celebrity guests are filmed or interviewed only with the approval of their teams. The location is private and may not be published.

About SKVLK Fest

SKVLK Fest is a Los Angeles youth-culture music and carnival festival founded by Anelise, the daughter of Academy Award winner Jamie Foxx. Its 2025 edition drew 25,000 guests and reached more than 100 million people, according to the festival.
