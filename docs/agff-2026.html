The festival

Stories of hope belong in Hollywood.

For three days in Beverly Hills, the Authentic Global Film Festival brings purpose-driven cinema from around the world to the Writers Guild Theater. More than 950 films from roughly 30 countries were submitted this year. The festival champions films that promote peace, family, faith, diversity, inclusion and environmental stewardship, with emerging filmmakers of every age sharing the room with Oscar-winning talent.

“More than 950 filmmakers from around the world trusted us with their stories this year. Many of them have never seen their work on a big screen,” said Dr. Gershom Sikaala, Founder and Executive Director. “Talent doesn’t depend on where you were born or how old you are, but opportunity too often does. I founded this festival to close that gap, and to prove that stories of hope belong in Hollywood.”

Oscar Winner Nick Vallelonga, DeVon Franklin and Richard T. Jones Lead the Red Carpet as the Authentic Global Film Festival & Awards Bring 30 Nations to Beverly Hills, Nov. 5–7

What
The Authentic Global Film Festival & Global Film Awards 2026: three days of screenings, panels and a red-carpet awards gala honoring purpose-driven, faith-and-family and international cinema. More than 950 films from roughly 30 countries were submitted.
When
Thursday, Nov. 5 – Opening day: screenings and arrivals

Saturday, Nov. 7 – Red carpet 5–7 PM · Awards ceremony 7–9 PM · Gala dinner to follow
Where
Writers Guild Theater, 135 S. Doheny Drive, Beverly Hills, CA 90211
Who
Two-time Oscar winner Nick Vallelonga (Green Book); producer DeVon Franklin; Richard T. Interviews
Red-carpet interviews with talent, honorees and the festival founder are available on Saturday, 5–7 PM. One-on-one requests may be arranged in advance through PR STARPOWER.
Why it matters
In a year when Hollywood is debating who gets a seat at the table, AGFF puts filmmakers from underrepresented communities, including people of color, women, and the neurodiverse and disability communities, in the same room as Oscar winners.
