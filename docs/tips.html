Confidential Send us a tip

Seen something the world should know?

Tell our editors. Your tip goes straight from your own email app to the World Desk. This website does not record, store or see anything you type.

Before you write: stay safe

  • Use your own phone or computer and a personal email account, not a work device, work email or office network.
  • Ordinary email is not fully private. If your tip is sensitive, send a short first message asking us for a safer way to talk, and leave the details out until we reply.
  • Do not put yourself or anyone else in danger, and do not break the law to get information for us.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Your tip

Pressing the button opens a new email to info@worldnewswirepress.com in your own mail app with your words filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there. Prefer to write it yourself? Email info@worldnewswirepress.com.

What we promise

  • We protect our sources. We never publish who you are without your clear permission.
  • An editor reads every tip. We check what you tell us independently before we publish anything.
  • We will not pay for tips, and we will not promise a story. We will tell you honestly if we cannot pursue it.
